## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted for high-volume commerce, prioritizing efficiency, trust, and frictionless transactions. The aesthetic bridges utilitarian functionality with approachable consumer retail standards. 

- **Brand Personality:** Trustworthy, efficient, accessible, and direct.
- **Target Audience:** Digital consumers seeking vast selection, competitive pricing, and rapid fulfillment.
- **Emotional Response:** Confidence, security, and effortless discovery.
- **Design Style:** Corporate / Modern, optimized for density, readability, and rapid scanning.

## Colors

The color palette establishes immediate retail recognition through authoritative dark navy structural elements and high-conversion warm orange interactions. 

- **Primary (`#FF9900`):** Reserved strictly for primary calls-to-action, pricing emphasis, and conversion highlights.
- **Secondary (`#131921`):** Applied to app headers, navigation bars, and primary dark surfaces to ground the interface.
- **Tertiary (`#232F3E`):** Used for sub-headers, dark mode accents, and secondary navigation elements.
- **Neutral (`#EAEDED` & `#FFFFFF`):** Provides the foundational canvas. Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) forms distinct card surfaces, while light gray (`#EAEDED`) acts as the background separator for high readability and content partitioning.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes extreme legibility across varied mobile viewports. Using Inter throughout establishes a clean, systematic, and utilitarian information hierarchy.

- Scale tightly controls density, ensuring dense product listings, pricing matrices, and review snippets remain scannable without visual fatigue.
- Font weights are strictly managed to separate navigational chrome (bold) from descriptive product data (regular).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model relies on a structured 12-column fluid grid adapted for mobile viewports, emphasizing compact, horizontal product carousels and vertical list feeds.

- **Margins & Gutters:** Outer canvas margins are set to a consistent `16px`, with `12px` gutters separating card grids to maximize screen real estate for merchandise.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px baseline grid scale, ensuring predictable, scannable vertical rhythms between product titles, ratings, pricing, and fulfillment badges.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on low-contrast outlines and clean surface-container separation rather than heavy drop shadows, preserving a flat, high-performance mobile UI.

- **Surfaces:** Pure white product cards sit on light neutral backgrounds (`#EAEDED`), separated by subtle 1px borders (`#D5DBDB`) to define touch targets.
- **Floating Elements:** Sticky bottom checkout bars and floating filters use minimal, diffused ambient shadows to lift them above the scrolling content without adding visual noise.

## Shapes

A soft shape language balances approachability with industrial precision. 

- **Radii:** Standard UI elements feature a `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radius, projecting a functional, reliable retail tool. Larger containers (`rounded-lg`) use `0.5rem` (`8px`) for modal dialogs and promotional banners.
- **Badges & Pills:** Category filters and discount tags utilize pill-shaped radiuses to clearly demarcate interactive filters from static content.

## Components

Components are engineered for high-intent conversion and rapid scanning.

- **Buttons:** Primary CTA buttons utilize the signature warm orange (`#FF9900`) with dark charcoal text for maximum contrast. Secondary buttons feature neutral white backgrounds with dark borders.
- **Input Fields:** Search bars and form inputs feature a crisp 1px border, high-contrast placeholder text, and integrated clear/scan action icons on the trailing edge.
- **Cards:** Product cards feature a clean white surface, pinned image aspect ratios, truncated two-line titles, prominent pricing typography, and star-rating rows.
- **Chips & Filters:** Horizontal scrollable chip groups for refined attribute selection (e.g., Prime eligible, brand, price range).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** High-contrast square checkboxes and circular radio inputs optimized for dense filter drawers and checkout options.
- **Lists:** Structured list rows with leading thumbnails, multi-line metadata, and trailing chevron indicators for order history and settings menus.
